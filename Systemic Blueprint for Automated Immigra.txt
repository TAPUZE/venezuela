Regulatory bodies and state bar associations actively pursue enforcement against software entities crossing this threshold, leading to severe financial penalties and operational shutdown.2.1 The Boundary Between Legal Information and UPLAn automated WhatsApp intake bot must strictly function as a data-gathering, translation, and mapping utility. Providing legal information includes pointing users to exact form instructions, reading or translating published USCIS requirements, and transcribing user-provided factual data directly into corresponding PDF coordinates.Conversely, legal advice—which triggers UPL liability—occurs when the software, an LLM agent, or an uncredentialed human operator analyzes a user's specific factual matrix, applies legal principles to those facts, and recommends a specific course of action. For example, the bot cannot evaluate a user's narrative and assert, "Based on your timeline, you qualify for the 'extraordinary circumstances' exception to the one-year deadline." It may only present the statutory definitions of those exceptions and ask the user to select which, if any, they believe applies to their timeline.The platform must train its LLM agents (specifically the Intake Director and Mistake Vault) via rigorous prompt engineering to refuse predictive analysis regarding the likelihood of success of a claim or the strategic selection of specific relief mechanisms. The Mistake Vault agent may flag contradictory dates between a user's uploaded passport and their chat input, but it must not advise the user on how to legally justify the contradiction.2.2 DOJ/EOIR Recognition & Accreditation ParalysisThe optimal operational model for an immigration technology non-profit is to obtain Recognition and Accreditation (R&A) from the Department of Justice (DOJ) and the Executive Office for Immigration Review (EOIR). This designation permits non-attorney staff (Accredited Representatives) to legally represent clients before USCIS and immigration courts. To qualify, an organization must establish itself as a federally tax-exempt non-profit and demonstrate that it provides services primarily to low-income individuals.However, the platform cannot rely on the rapid onboarding of new Accredited Representatives to scale its review dashboard. In March 2026, the federal government functionally gutted the R&A program infrastructure by abruptly reassigning all staff attorneys tasked with adjudicating applications. While applications technically remain open, there is no internal staff to approve new organizations, accredit new representatives, or even process routine renewals required to maintain good standing. Consequently, the platform's attorney-review dashboard must route cases exclusively to pre-existing, licensed pro-bono attorneys or already-accredited representatives whose credentials have not yet lapsed.2.3 State-Level Form Preparer Constraints: California and FloridaBecause the platform essentially operates as an automated "immigration consultant" or form preparer in the jurisdictions where it is accessed via mobile devices, it must comply with aggressive state-level regulations enacted to curb "notario" fraud.Florida (HB 915 / SB 846):
